## Getting Started

*Anchor: `id="getting-started"`*

`GETTING STARTED`

### What is TotalRecalls?

TotalRecalls is a Windows app that saves your AI conversations to your own PC. It downloads chats from ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, Grok, DeepSeek, Mistral, and Qwen. Each chat is saved as a Markdown file and a JSON file.

Every chat lands in one local Library folder, sorted by provider. You end up with a single archive of your AI work that lives on your drive instead of inside eight separate sidebars.

### How do I get started with TotalRecalls?

Download the free version, and you can save your first chats in four steps:

1. Download TotalRecalls from totalrecalls.app.
2. Unzip the file and run the app on your Windows PC.
3. Pick a provider and sign in to it inside the app.
4. Choose your conversations and download them.

Your chats appear in `C:\TotalRecalls\Library\`. If Windows shows a SmartScreen prompt on first launch, [here's how to continue](/smartscreen/).

### Do I need to create a TotalRecalls account?

No. TotalRecalls has no account, no sign-up, and no email list to join before you use it.

The only sign-ins happen inside the app, when you sign in to each AI provider you want to save from. That happens locally, on your PC. You can start using TotalRecalls as soon as you unzip it.

### What do I need before I start?

You need three things:

- A PC running Windows 10 or 11
- An internet connection for downloading chats
- Your existing account with each AI provider you want to save from

The Free Tier needs no payment card. If you have those three things, you're set.

## Providers

*Anchor: `id="providers"`*

`PROVIDERS`

### Which AI providers does TotalRecalls support?

TotalRecalls supports eight providers: ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, Grok, DeepSeek, Mistral, and Qwen.

The Free Tier covers ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity. Pro covers all eight. Every provider saves to the same Library folder, so your whole AI history ends up in one place.

### Which providers are included in the Free Tier?

The Free Tier includes ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity, with up to 5 conversations each.

Gemini, Grok, DeepSeek, Mistral, and Qwen need Pro. The Free Tier is built as a real test drive, so you can see exactly how the app handles your own chats before you pay.

### How do I download my Gemini chat history?

Gemini chats come through Google Takeout, which TotalRecalls converts into Markdown and JSON files. Here's how it works:

1. Request your Gemini data through Google Takeout.
2. Download and extract the ZIP file when Google sends it.
3. Point TotalRecalls to the extracted folder.
4. TotalRecalls converts your chats and saves them in the `gemini\` subfolder of your Library.

Google controls how long a Takeout request takes. Gemini is included with Pro. For a full walkthrough, see the [Gemini Takeout guide](/guides/gemini-takeout-archive/).

### How do the other providers work?

The other seven providers are direct downloads. You sign in to the provider inside the app, choose your conversations, and TotalRecalls saves them to your Library.

Gemini is the only provider that uses a Google Takeout file. For provider-specific tips, see the [Guides](/guides/).

### Does TotalRecalls save DeepSeek R1 thinking traces?

Yes. When you download DeepSeek chats, TotalRecalls saves the R1 reasoning chains along with the conversation. They go into the same Markdown and JSON files.

That means you can revisit how the model reached an answer, not just the answer itself. DeepSeek is included with Pro.

### Will TotalRecalls add more AI providers?

New providers and capabilities are in development. We don't give release dates until something is ready to ship.

When a new provider launches, we'll announce it in the Release Notes on our website. Check there for the latest changes.

## Files & Ownership

*Anchor: `id="files"`*

`FILES & OWNERSHIP`

### Where does TotalRecalls save my chats?

Your chats are saved in `C:\TotalRecalls\Library\`, with one subfolder per provider.

For example, a Claude chat might land at `claude\2026-07-28-refactor-plan.md`. Each file name starts with the date, so your Library sorts itself by time. Everything stays on your own drive.

### What file formats does TotalRecalls use?

Every conversation is saved in two formats:

- **Markdown (`.md`):** Plain text with light formatting, easy to read in any editor
- **JSON (`.json`):** Structured data you can load into other tools or scripts

Both are open, standard formats, and neither one is proprietary. Any program that reads plain text can open them.

### What can I open my saved chats with?

You can open them with almost anything. Markdown files open in Notepad, VS Code, and most notes apps. JSON files open in any text editor or code tool.

You don't need TotalRecalls installed to read your files. If a program can open plain text, it can open your chats.

### Can I search my saved chats?

Yes. All your chats sit in one folder, so you can search every provider at once.

Use your favorite editor's folder search, a desktop search tool, or any app that searches text files. Instead of checking eight separate chat apps, you search one Library.

### Can I move my library to a new PC or back it up?

Yes. Copy the Library folder to a new PC, an external drive, or your own backup location. That's the whole move.

Your files don't need a login or a license to open. For more on keeping your files portable, see [The Ownership Path](/ownership/).

### What happens to my files if I stop using TotalRecalls?

Your files stay exactly where they are. They're ordinary Markdown and JSON files on your drive, so they keep working with or without the app.

Uninstalling TotalRecalls doesn't touch your Library. Your saved chats remain yours to open, move, or delete.

### Do I own the files TotalRecalls creates?

The files live on your drive, and you control them. You can copy, back up, or delete them whenever you like.

The content of each chat still follows each AI provider's own terms. Our [Terms of Service](/terms/) explain your responsibility to follow them.

## Privacy

*Anchor: `id="privacy"`*

`PRIVACY`

### Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. TotalRecalls runs on your PC, and your conversations aren't sent to our servers.

When you download chats, the app connects directly to each AI provider and saves the results to your drive. There's no cloud library and no copy stored with us.

### How does signing in to AI providers work?

You sign in to each AI provider locally, inside the app. TotalRecalls doesn't host or transmit your session tokens.

Your sign-in stays between your PC and the provider. We never see your provider password or hold your session on a server.

### Does TotalRecalls collect my data?

We never collect, host, or transmit your AI conversations. TotalRecalls is local-first by design.

For full details, read our [Privacy Policy](/privacy/). It explains exactly what we do and don't handle.

### Does the site show ads?

No. This site sells a tool, not pageviews, so you won't see ad clutter.

Our business is the one-time Pro purchase and optional add-ons. That's the whole model.

## Technical

*Anchor: `id="technical"`*

`TECHNICAL`

### Which versions of Windows does TotalRecalls support?

TotalRecalls runs on Windows 10 and Windows 11.

It's a portable desktop app. You unzip it and run it, with no complicated installer. If your PC runs Windows 10 or 11, you can use TotalRecalls.

### Is there a Mac or Linux version?

Not yet. Versions for macOS and Linux are in development, and we don't have release dates to share.

The Free Tier doesn't run on a Mac either. If you're on macOS or Linux, please wait for your version rather than buy now.

### Can I use TotalRecalls on my phone?

No. TotalRecalls is a Windows desktop app, so it doesn't run on phones or tablets.

If you're browsing on mobile, open totalrecalls.app on your Windows PC to download it.

### Why does Windows say "Windows protected your PC"?

It's Microsoft Defender SmartScreen, and it's a reputation check, not a malware warning. TotalRecalls is new, so SmartScreen hasn't seen it often enough to recognize it yet.

To continue:

1. Click **More info** on the prompt.
2. Click **Run anyway**.

Only do this when your file came from totalrecalls.app. [Read the SmartScreen guide →](/smartscreen/)

### Does TotalRecalls work offline?

Partly. Opening, reading, and searching your saved chats works offline.

Downloading new chats needs an internet connection, because the app connects to each AI provider. Once a chat is saved, you can read it anywhere.

### What should I do if a download fails?

Most failed downloads come from an expired sign-in or a dropped connection. Try these steps:

1. Check your internet connection.
2. Sign in to the provider again inside the app.
3. Run the download again.

If it still fails, email <support@totalrecalls.app>. Include the provider name, your Windows version, and your TotalRecalls version so we can help faster.

### How do I contact support?

Email <support@totalrecalls.app>. TotalRecalls is built by one developer, and support email is answered personally.

For press inquiries, email <press@totalrecalls.app>.
