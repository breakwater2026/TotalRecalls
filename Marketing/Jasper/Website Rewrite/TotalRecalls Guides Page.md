### 2. Quick Start

*Anchor: `id="quick-start"`*

`START HERE`

## Your first download in four steps

The basic flow is the same for every provider. The guides below add the details that differ.

1. **Download and unzip.** Get TotalRecalls from totalrecalls.app and unzip it on your Windows 10 or 11 PC.
2. **Run the app.** Windows may show a SmartScreen prompt on first launch. [Here's what it means →](/smartscreen/)
3. **Pick a provider and sign in.** You sign in locally, inside the app. There's no TotalRecalls account.
4. **Choose your chats and download.** Your conversations land in your Library, sorted by provider.

`C:\TotalRecalls\Library\chatgpt\2026-08-01-pricing-research.md` · `● SAVED LOCALLY`

**Gemini works differently.** Gemini history doesn't download directly. You request a Google Takeout archive, extract it, and point TotalRecalls to the folder. ### 5. Quick Fixes

*Anchor: `id="quick-fixes"`*

`QUICK FIXES`

## Stuck? Try these first

**Windows shows "Windows protected your PC."**\
That's SmartScreen checking a new app's reputation. Click More info, then Run anyway, when your file came from totalrecalls.app. [Read the SmartScreen guide →](/smartscreen/)

**A download failed or stopped.**\
Check your connection, sign in to the provider again inside the app, and run the download again.

**Your Gemini Takeout hasn't arrived.**\
Google sends Takeout archives on its own schedule. Once the ZIP arrives, extract it and follow the Gemini guide.

**Still stuck?**\
Email <support@totalrecalls.app> with the provider name, your Windows version, and your TotalRecalls version.

### Behavior

- **Static by default.** No carousels, tickers, filters that animate, or countdowns.
- **Optional reveal:** Cards fade in with an 8px upward slide over 200ms, staggered 40ms apart, triggered once on scroll. Disable under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Jump chips:** Each chip scrolls to its card with a 96px offset and briefly sets a Steel Blue border on that card for 1 second. No flashing.
- **Deep links:** Give each card an ID (`#chatgpt`, `#claude`, `#perplexity`, `#gemini`, `#grok`, `#deepseek`, `#mistral`, `#qwen`) for support replies and social posts.
- **Accessible cards:** Render each card as one link with the card title as its accessible name. Chips and topic lists sit inside the link as plain text, with no nested links.
- **Mobile file path:** Wrap at backslashes or truncate the middle. Never shrink mono text below 12px.
- **Structured data:** Add `CollectionPage` with an `ItemList` of the nine guide URLs.
- **Icons:** Optional 24px line icons in Mist on the Quick Start steps (download tray, window, sign-in, folder). No cloud, shield, or lock icons. No provider logos or brand colors. Refer to providers by name only.
