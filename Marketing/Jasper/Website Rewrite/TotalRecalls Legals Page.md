### 4. Your License

*Anchor: `id="license"`*

`LICENSE`

## What your license allows

When you buy Pro, you get a one-time, perpetual digital license to use TotalRecalls. You pay once, and the license doesn't expire.

**It's for one person.**\
The license belongs to you as an individual user. You can activate it on up to three devices you use, such as a desktop, a laptop, and a work machine.

**Please keep your key to yourself.**\
Don't share, resell, or publish your license key, and don't redistribute the app. If a device breaks or you replace a PC, email support and we'll help you move an activation.

**You're licensing the software, not buying it outright.**\
TotalRecalls is closed-source software. The app itself stays our property. Please don't copy, modify, or reverse engineer it.

**Your saved files stay with you.**\
The Markdown and JSON files TotalRecalls creates live on your own drive. They keep working with or without the app, and you can copy them anywhere without a license.

### 5. Privacy Summary

*Anchor: `id="privacy"`*

`PRIVACY`

## Local-first, by design

TotalRecalls runs on your Windows PC. We built it so your AI conversations never pass through us.

**What TotalRecalls does:**

- Runs locally on your computer
- Lets you sign in to each AI provider locally, inside the app
- Connects directly to each provider to download your chats
- Saves your chats as files in your own Library folder

**What TotalRecalls doesn't do:**

- Send your conversations to our servers
- Host, collect, or transmit your AI conversations
- Host or transmit your provider sign-in sessions
- Ask you to create a TotalRecalls account

**What we do receive:**

- **When you buy Pro,** our checkout provider shares your name, email address, and order details with us so we can deliver and support your license.
- **When you activate Pro,** the app verifies your license key so it can apply the 3-device limit.
- **When you email us,** we receive whatever you choose to send, and we use it only to answer you.

We don't sell your information, and this site doesn't run ads. [Read the full Privacy Policy →](/privacy/)

`100% LOCAL` · `NO CLOUD UPLOAD` · `NO ACCOUNT`

### 6. Terms of Service Summary

*Anchor: `id="terms"`*

`TERMS`

## The main points of our terms

**One-time purchase.**\
Pro is a single purchase with no recurring charge. Delivery is digital: your license key arrives by email right after checkout.

**Single-user license.**\
Each license covers one person, on up to three devices.

**You follow each provider's terms.**\
TotalRecalls downloads your own conversations, using your own sign-in. You're responsible for following the terms of use of every AI provider you download from. How you use each chat's content also follows that provider's terms.

**Providers can change things.**\
AI providers update their sites and apps without notice. A change on their side can pause downloads for that provider. We work to restore support through app updates, but we can't promise that any provider will stay available or work the same way forever.

**Gemini works differently.**\
Gemini history comes through a Google Takeout archive, which TotalRecalls converts to Markdown and JSON. Google controls how and when Takeout archives arrive.

**Names and trademarks.**\
ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, Grok, DeepSeek, Mistral, and Qwen are trademarks of their owners. TotalRecalls isn't affiliated with or endorsed by any of them. We use their names only to describe which services the app works with.

*Anchor: `id="platforms"`*

`PLATFORMS`

## Where TotalRecalls runs today

**Windows 10 and 11 only.**\
TotalRecalls is a portable Windows desktop app. You unzip it and run it on your PC.

**macOS and Linux are in development.**\

Because TotalRecalls is new, Windows SmartScreen may show "Windows protected your PC" on first launch. It's a reputation check, not a malware warning. Click More info, then Run anyway, when your file came from totalrecalls.app. [Read the SmartScreen guide →](/smartscreen/)

**Download only from us.**\
Get TotalRecalls only from totalrecalls.app. We can't support copies from anywhere else.

### Behavior

- **No Archive Amber on this page.** Legal reference stays neutral. The only buttons are a Secondary "Try it free" and a Tertiary pricing link at the bottom.
- **No Local Green on this page.** Nothing here shows a saved state, so every icon and chip stays in Mist, Dust, or Steel Blue.
- **Static.** No fade-ins, counters, or countdowns. Readers scan legal pages, so content appears immediately.
- **Deep links:** Keep `id="at-a-glance"`, `id="pricing"`, `id="license"`, `id="privacy"`, `id="terms"`, `id="refunds"`, `id="platforms"`, and `id="contact"`. Jump chips scroll with a 96px offset. Headings show a copyable link icon on hover for support replies.
- **Print-friendly:** Add a print stylesheet with a white background, black text, hidden navigation, and full URLs after links.
- **Last updated date:** Store it as one variable and show it in the hero chip, the footer of this page, and the full Privacy Policy and Terms, so all three change together.
- **Icons:** Info-circle only. No shields, locks, warning triangles, cloud icons, or provider logos.
- **Accessibility:** Use real `<table>` markup with header cells for the At a Glance table. The sticky table of contents is a `<nav>` with `aria-label="On this page"`.
