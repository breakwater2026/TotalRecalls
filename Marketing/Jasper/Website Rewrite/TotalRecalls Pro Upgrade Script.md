# TotalRecalls Pro Upgrade Script

**Format:** In-app upgrade prompt and web video\
**Runtime:** About 75 seconds\
**Voice:** Calm, confident, and direct. Speak at an unhurried pace, with short pauses between beats.\
**Look:** Obsidian background, Paper text, Steel Blue file paths in mono type, and Archive Amber reserved for the price and final CTA.

---

## Scene 1: The Free Tier Ceiling

**Time:** 0:00–0:10

**VISUAL**

- Open on the TotalRecalls Library folder at `C:\TotalRecalls\Library\`.
- The `chatgpt\` subfolder holds exactly five `.md` files.
- A small mono counter in the corner reads `5 / 5`.
- The cursor hovers over the folder, then pauses.

**ON-SCREEN TEXT**\
`FREE TIER · 5 CONVERSATIONS PER PROVIDER`

**VOICEOVER**\
You've tried TotalRecalls. Five chats each from ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity, saved as files on your own PC.

## Production Notes

- **Spoken word count:** About 170 words, which fits the 60–90 second window at a calm pace.
- **Music:** Soft, low ambient bed with no build-ups, drops, or swells.
- **Motion:** Slow and deliberate. No flashing, countdowns, or urgency cues.
- **Providers:** Show names as text only. Don't use official logos, brand colors, or real chat interfaces.
- **Screen captures:** Blur all account names, emails, and license keys.
- **Accuracy:** Keep "launch price" beside every mention of $24, and update the video when the price changes. Before recording Scene 6, confirm that a Free Tier install upgrades to Pro with the license key and doesn't need a separate download.
