## Claim-wording conflicts still live in this folder

These are the traps. The Master Plan flags the first two as launch blockers.

1. **Free-tier limit.** ✅ **RESOLVED 2026-11-15.** **3 providers (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity) × 5
   conversations each = 15 maximum**, confirmed against the live site. The Gen D assets (Launch Plan,
   Upload Pack, 4-Week Social Pack) already carry the correct "5 conversations each" wording. The
   copied Gen A/B/C documents still say "per download" / "per provider" — superseded; do not quote
   them for tier wording.
2. **Pricing.** $24 Pro everywhere; Gen D adds a $49 Pro+SSE bundle (regular $98). Any asset that
   says only "$24" is incomplete, not wrong.
3. **Add-ons.** Master Plan says semantic search / Obsidian sync are "not established — do not
   market them." Gen D markets the Smart Search Engine as a live product. Gen D is correct.
4. **Platform.** "Windows 10/11 today; Linux and macOS in the works" — consistent everywhere, but
   several older assets omit it.
5. **Provider tos.** The Master Plan requires the provider-terms reply to be reviewed before use.
   That review is not evidenced anywhere in the folder.
