## 2. Store Listing Details

### Description
```text
TotalRecalls is the private, on-device archiver that backs up your AI conversation histories into clean, offline Markdown and JSON files on your PC.

Tired of losing your prompt engineering work, research threads, and code snippets inside cloud silos? TotalRecalls lets you export your complete conversation histories directly to your local drive.

KEY FEATURES:
• Multi-Provider Support: Connect to ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity (Free Tier). Upgrade to Pro to unlock Gemini, Grok, DeepSeek, Mistral, and Qwen Chat.
• 100% Local & Private: Sign in directly on your computer. Your session tokens, prompts, and conversation data never touch third-party cloud servers.
• Universal Formats: Every conversation is saved as both human-readable Markdown (.md) with code blocks intact, and structured JSON (.json) ready for local search, RAG pipelines, Obsidian, or Notion.
• Offline Freedom: Own your chat data forever. Search and browse your library completely offline without depending on AI platform availability.

FREE TIER & PRO LICENSING:
The Microsoft Store edition provides full Free Tier access out of the box, allowing you to test and export up to 5 conversations across ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity at no cost. 

To export all conversations with zero download caps and unlock all 8 AI providers (including Gemini, Grok, DeepSeek, Mistral, and Qwen), purchase a Downloader Pro license key from our official website (https://totalrecalls.app) and activate it instantly inside the app.
```
