## TotalRecalls Main Product Explainer — Video Outline

Title
"Recall Every AI Conversation" — TotalRecalls Product Explainer
Objective
Drive Pro license purchases ($24 USD, one-time, discounted launch price) by demonstrating how TotalRecalls solves the problem of lost AI conversation history, while building trust through its 100% local, privacy-first design.
Target Audience
Knowledge workers, developers, researchers, and writers who use multiple AI assistants daily and are privacy-conscious or have already lost a valuable conversation. Primary platforms: YouTube, Instagram Reels, and Meta (Facebook/Instagram Feed).
Key Messages
AI conversations disappear without warning — and most people aren't backing them up.
TotalRecalls downloads chats from 8 major providers into one local folder, forever.
Everything happens locally — zero cloud upload, no account required.
One-time $24 launch price (50% off), with a free tier to try first.
Available now for Windows 10/11 — mobile viewers must visit totalrecalls.app on a PC.
Duration
Total runtime: 75 seconds (fits the 45–90 sec range for this format)
Video Outline
Introduction (0:00–0:12)
Open on the "Live Feed" ticker visual — real-looking log entries scrolling past: "chatgpt · download failed · thread lost," "claude · ui revision · 4 threads orphaned," "chatgpt · 9 months of prompts · no backup."
Voiceover: "Every day, AI conversations disappear. A session expires. A UI changes. A tab closes. And just like that — months of work, gone."
Visuals/Audio: Dark background, scrolling log ticker with a subtle glitch/flicker effect on each "lost" entry. Low, tense ambient audio bed. Text overlays highlight key phrases ("thread lost," "no backup") in red.
Main Content
Segment 1: The Problem (0:12–0:22)
Voiceover: "You back up your photos. You back up your files. But your AI conversations? Chances are, they're one error message away from vanishing forever."
Visuals/Audio: Split-screen showing a folder icon labeled "Photos — Backed Up" next to a fading/dissolving chat bubble labeled "AI Chats — Not Backed Up." Audio bed shifts to a more contemplative tone.
Segment 2: The Solution Reveal (0:22–0:32)
Voiceover: "Meet TotalRecalls — a Windows app that downloads your conversations from ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, Grok, DeepSeek, Mistral, and Qwen into one private folder on your own computer."
Visuals/Audio: Logo animation reveal (blue brain in speech bubble). Provider icons animate in from all sides, converging into a single folder icon labeled "Library." Audio swells slightly, more upbeat and confident.
Segment 3: The Demo (0:32–0:55)
Voiceover: "Pick a provider. Sign in. Hit download. Your conversations land as Markdown and JSON files, ready to open, search, or store however you like."
Visuals/Audio: Screen-capture footage from the Claude demo: Step 1 (choose provider) → Step 2 (connect account) → Step 3 (files populating in File Explorer) → completed download with file summary. Show a downloaded Markdown file opening in Word, scrolling through formatted conversation text. Light, upbeat background music continues; on-screen captions reinforce each step ("Select," "Connect," "Download," "Done").
Segment 4: Trust & Proof (0:55–1:03)
Voiceover: "No cloud. No account. No uploads. Everything stays on your machine — 100% local, by design."
Visuals/Audio: Visual badge overlay: "100% Local · Zero Cloud Upload." Show the "This PC → New PC" file migration graphic to reinforce portability. Audio tone remains confident and steady.
Conclusion (1:03–1:15)
Voiceover: "Try TotalRecalls free today. Or unlock every provider and unlimited downloads for a one-time $24 — 50% off the launch price. No subscription. Ever."
Visuals/Audio: Pricing card visual: "$24 USD · one-time · lifetime license · discounted launch price." End card with logo, URL (totalrecalls.app), and button-style text overlays: "Try Free" and "Buy Now." Music resolves to a clean, confident final note.
Call to Action (on-screen text + voiceover): "Visit totalrecalls.app on your Windows PC to get started." (For mobile placements, reinforce verbally and on-screen that viewers need to visit the site from a desktop — never direct to an app store.)
