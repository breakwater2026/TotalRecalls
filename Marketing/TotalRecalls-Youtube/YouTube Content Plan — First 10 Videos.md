How to sequence the uploads
Order matters as much as content. Here's a simple four-week rollout that keeps momentum without overwhelming a solo operator.
Week 1: Set the foundation
Publish Video 1 (explainer), Video 2 (founder story), and Video 3 (loss-aversion Short). Together they explain what the product does, who built it, and why viewers should care.
Week 2: Capture search intent
Post Video 4 (ChatGPT walkthrough) and Video 6 (provider friction Short). ChatGPT gets the most searches, so lead with it.
Week 3: Expand coverage and handle doubts
Publish Video 5 (Claude walkthrough) and Video 8 (safety). The safety video quietly removes the biggest barrier for a new desktop app.
Week 4: Broaden reach and push urgency
Post Video 7 (Perplexity/Gemini), Video 9 (Free vs Pro), and Video 10 (urgency Short) as the discount window closes.
Takeaway: Front-load trust, then use search and urgency to convert warmed-up viewers.
Quick tips to get more from each video
A few habits will stretch these 10 videos much further.
Hook in 3 seconds. Open with the pain, the product working, or a challenged assumption.
Repurpose long-form into Shorts. Each walkthrough can yield two or three vertical clips.
Keep CTAs consistent. Use the same "visit from desktop" line in the video, description, and pinned comment.
Write clear, intent-driven titles. Match what people actually type into search.
Build playlists early. Group videos by provider and by "Start Here" so the channel reads as a proof library.
