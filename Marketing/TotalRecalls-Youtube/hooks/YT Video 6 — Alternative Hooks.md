# Video 6 Alternative Hooks

Each hook below replaces Scene 1 (0:00–0:05) and hands off directly to Scene 2, "Option one: the official export." Every option opens cold on the first spoken word, runs about 5 seconds, and keeps the dry, understated tone that makes the original dare work.

---

## At a Glance

| Option | Title           | Core Move                                   | Best For                                |
| ------ | --------------- | ------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| 1      | The Stopwatch   | A timed challenge the viewer can't win      | Retention and replays                   |
| 2      | The Eulogy      | A mock funeral for a lost chat              | Emotional recognition of the pain       |

## Hook Option 1: The Stopwatch

**\[VO / Founder, calm and slightly smug]**\
Bet you can't find last month's best AI chat in ten seconds. Go on.\
**\[pause: half a beat]**\
I'm timing you.

*(17 words)*

**\[On-screen text]** (center, bold white, pops in word by word)\
FIND IT IN 10 SECONDS.\
GO.

**\[Visual]**\
Medium close-up, founder framed in the upper two-thirds. The founder holds a phone stopwatch toward the camera, thumb hovering. On "Go on," the thumb taps Start. A small countdown chip appears in the upper left and ticks down from 10. The founder settles back and crosses their arms, unbothered.

**\[SFX]**\
A crisp stopwatch click on "Go on," then soft ticking under "I'm timing you." The ticking continues faintly into Scene 2 and cuts out on the error "bonk."

**\[Why it works]**\
It turns a passive viewer into a participant. Most people instantly picture themselves scrolling through a crowded sidebar, and the countdown gives them a reason to stay for the full 10 seconds, which carries them past the drop-off point.

**\[Loop note]**\
End Scene 7 with the founder holding the stopwatch up again, thumb hovering. The countdown chip resets to 10 on the loop.

## Hook Option 2: The Eulogy

**\[VO / Founder, solemn and deadpan]**\
Here lies your best AI thread. Lost to a closed tab. Nine months of prompts.\
**\[pause: one beat]**\
No backup.

*(17 words)*

**\[On-screen text]** (center, serif font, fades up in gray, then snaps to bold white on "No backup")\
RIP\
YOUR BEST AI THREAD\
2025–2026

**\[Visual]**\
Founder at the desk, head slightly bowed, hands folded. A single small candle sits beside the laptop. The laptop screen shows a blank, generic chat window with no provider branding. On "No backup," the founder looks up at the camera with one eyebrow raised, breaking the solemn mood just enough to signal the joke.

**\[SFX]**\
One low, soft bell tone on "Here lies." Silence through the rest of the line. A faint candle-snuff "fft" on "No backup" as the candle goes out.

**\[Why it works]**\
It names a loss most AI users have felt but rarely say out loud. The over-the-top funeral framing keeps it playful, so the pain point lands without sounding like a scare tactic.

**\[Loop note]**\
End Scene 7 with the founder relighting the candle and folding their hands, matching the opening pose.

## Hook Option 3: Two Bad Options

**\[VO / Founder, dry and matter-of-fact]**\
There are two ways to save your AI chats forever.\
**\[pause: half a beat]**\
Both are terrible. Stick around for the third.

*(17 words)*

**\[On-screen text]** (upper third, step-chip style)\
2 WAYS TO SAVE YOUR AI CHATS.\
BOTH TERRIBLE.

**\[Visual]**\
Medium close-up, founder framed in the upper two-thirds. The founder holds up two fingers on "two ways." On "Both are terrible," the fingers wobble into a so-so gesture while the founder gives a slight grimace. On "Stick around for the third," a third finger starts to rise, then the shot hard cuts to Scene 2 before it fully lifts.

**\[SFX]**\
Two short game-show "wrong answer" buzzers, one on each finger wobble. A quick whoosh on the hard cut into Scene 2.

**\[Why it works]**\
It sets up the exact structure of the video. Scene 2 becomes "option one," Scene 3 becomes "option two," and Scene 4 pays off the promised third way. The unfinished finger raise creates an open loop that viewers want to see closed.

**\[Loop note]**\
End Scene 7 with the founder holding up two fingers again, matching the first frame. The replay feels like a fresh start to the same setup.

## Production Notes for All Three Hooks

- **Keep the rest of the video unchanged.** Each hook slots into Scene 1's 0:00–0:05 window, so Scenes 2 through 7 stay as written.

- **Adjust the Scene 6 callback.** "Still waiting" ties back to the original dare. Swap it to match the hook you choose:

  - **Option 1:** "Time's up." (glance at the stopwatch)
  - **Option 2:** "Rest easy." (small nod toward the candle)
  - **Option 3:** "That's the third." (third finger finally raised)

- **Hold the pauses.** The humor comes from timing. Let each beat feel slightly too long.

- **No real provider screens.** Any laptop screen visible in the hook should show a blank, generic interface.

- **No music for the first 2 seconds.** Let the VO and SFX carry the opening.

- **Test with a split run.** Publish the original and one alternative a week apart, then compare 3-second hold rate and average view duration before choosing a long-term winner.
