## Production Notes

### Claim Accuracy

- **"Three clicks" applies only to providers you're already signed into.** Before recording, sign in to the demo ChatGPT or Claude account inside TotalRecalls. That way, the on-camera flow is truly open, pick, download.
- **Test on the current build.** Walk through the flow click by click before you record. If it takes more than three clicks, even when signed in, change the VO to "a few clicks" and remove the numbered counter.
- **Don't imply first-time use takes three clicks.** Leave the sign-in step out of this video. Don't hide it with a jump cut.
- **"No account" means no TotalRecalls account.** There's no sign-up and no email field in the app. Viewers still sign in to their own AI provider locally.
- **No Gemini in Scene 4.** Gemini uses a Google Takeout-to-Markdown conversion, not a direct download.
- **Platform:** TotalRecalls runs on Windows 10 and 11 only. Keep "Windows app — visit from your desktop" in the VO, end card, caption, and pinned comment.
- **Free Tier limits:** Always say ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity, five conversations each.
- **Pricing:** Always say "$24 one-time" and label it as the 50% off launch price. Update the end card, caption, and pinned comment when the launch price ends.

### Visual Safety

- Build Scenes 2 and 3 entirely from abstract graphics. Never show a sharp shot of any real provider's interface, logo, export page, or email.
- Blur account names, email addresses, and personal details in the Scene 4 and 5 screen footage.
- Only TotalRecalls, Windows folders, Notepad, and Windows Search should appear sharp.
- Use non-sensitive demo chats with realistic titles, such as `2026-07-28-refactor-plan.md`.

### Delivery

- Keep it dry. The humor comes from understatement, so don't oversell it.
- Hold the "I'll wait" pause a full beat. It should feel slightly too long.
- Keep the shrug and the watch glance small and natural.
- Record each line 2–3 times. Leave a 1–2 second pause between sentences for clean cuts.

### Framing and Continuity

- Shoot natively in 9:16, with your eyes in the top third of the frame.
- Take a continuity photo of the opening pose. The loop frame must match it exactly: arm position, expression, camera height, and lighting.
- Keep the laptop in the same spot for every founder shot.

### Captions

- Place captions in the middle third, 2–4 words per line, synced word by word.
- Use bold white text with a subtle dark outline.
- Proofread brand names: TotalRecalls, ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity.
- Keep all text clear of the right-side action buttons and the bottom caption bar.

### Audio and Music

- No music for the first 2 seconds.
- Restless, low pulse under Scenes 2 and 3.
- Warm, steady bed from Scene 4 to the end.
- End the music on a clean cut so the loop sounds seamless.

### Color

- Cool gray-blue for Scenes 2 and 3 (the problem).
- Warm amber from Scene 4 onward (the solution).
- Founder shots stay neutral and consistent throughout.

### Hook Option 1: The Stopwatch

**\[VO / Founder, calm and slightly smug]**\
Bet you can't find last month's best AI chat in ten seconds. Go on.\
**\[pause: half a beat]**\
I'm timing you.

*(17 words)*

**\[On-screen text]** (center, bold white, pops in word by word)\
FIND IT IN 10 SECONDS.\
GO.

**\[Visual]**\
Medium close-up, founder framed in the upper two-thirds. The founder holds a phone stopwatch toward the camera, thumb hovering. On "Go on," the thumb taps Start. A small countdown chip appears in the upper left and ticks down from 10. The founder settles back and crosses their arms, unbothered.

**\[SFX]**\
A crisp stopwatch click on "Go on," then soft ticking under "I'm timing you." The ticking continues faintly into Scene 2 and cuts out on the error "bonk."

**\[Why it works]**\
It turns a passive viewer into a participant. Most people instantly picture themselves scrolling through a crowded sidebar, and the countdown gives them a reason to stay for the full 10 seconds, which carries them past the drop-off point.

**\[Loop note]**\
End Scene 7 with the founder holding the stopwatch up again, thumb hovering. The countdown chip resets to 10 on the loop.

Medium close-up, founder framed in the upper two-thirds. The founder holds up two fingers on "two ways." On "Both are terrible," the fingers wobble into a so-so gesture while the founder gives a slight grimace. On "Stick around for the third," a third finger starts to rise, then the shot hard cuts to Scene 2 before it fully lifts.

It sets up the exact structure of the video. Scene 2 becomes "option one," Scene 3 becomes "option two," and Scene 4 pays off the promised third way. The unfinished finger raise creates an open loop that viewers want to see closed.
