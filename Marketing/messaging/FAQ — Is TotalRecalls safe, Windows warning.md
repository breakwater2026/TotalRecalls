### Is TotalRecalls safe to use, and why does Windows show a warning when I install it?

Yes, TotalRecalls is safe — the "Windows protected your PC" message is a standard SmartScreen notice for newly released, independently developed apps, not a sign of malware. It appears because TotalRecalls hasn't yet built up a reputation history with Microsoft.

To proceed, click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. Only download TotalRecalls from the official site, totalrecalls.app, and this warning will fade over time as the app builds trust with Microsoft's systems.

### Is there a subscription, or does my data get uploaded to the cloud?

No to both — TotalRecalls has no subscription and no cloud upload. Pro is a one-time $24 USD purchase at a discounted launch price, with free updates to the core app for life.

The app also runs 100% locally: your ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, Grok, DeepSeek, Mistral, and Qwen conversations are saved directly to a folder on your own machine as Markdown and JSON files, and they're never transmitted to a server. There's no account or sign-up required either.

### What's the difference between the Free Tier and Pro, and which providers are supported?

The Free Tier lets you test TotalRecalls on 3 providers (ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity) with up to 5 conversations per download, while Pro unlocks all 8 providers with unlimited downloads for a one-time $24 USD fee.

TotalRecalls currently supports Windows 10 and 11, with macOS and Linux versions in development. Every download — whether Free or Pro — is saved in the same open, portable Markdown and JSON format, so you can try before you buy with zero risk.
