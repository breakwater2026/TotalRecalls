# TotalRecalls Launch Campaign

> Merged 2026-09-29 from documents for review — each section is one original document, unedited.


## Campaign Abstract


Goal: Drive awareness and one-time purchase conversions for TotalRecalls, a Windows desktop app that downloads and locally saves AI conversations from eight major providers.
Target Audience: The general public — anyone who regularly uses AI chatbots (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, Perplexity, Mistral, Qwen, or DeepSeek) and has felt the sting of a lost thread, an expired session, or a UI update that buried months of work. No technical expertise required; the audience spans casual users to power users across all ages.
Key Message: Your AI conversations are valuable and disappearing faster than you think. TotalRecalls gives you a simple, private way to download and permanently keep your chats from all eight major AI providers — stored locally as Markdown and JSON files on your own machine, with no cloud upload, no subscription, and no account required. Pay once ($24, discounted launch price), own it forever.
Every asset below reinforces three pillars: ownership (your files, your machine, forever), convenience (one app, eight providers), and freedom from subscriptions (a single $24 purchase, no recurring fees).


## TotalRecalls Launch Campaign


### Campaign Overview

TotalRecalls is entering the market with a clear mission: help everyday AI users protect the conversations they've come to rely on, before those conversations disappear for good. This 60-day launch campaign introduces TotalRecalls — a Windows desktop app that downloads and permanently saves AI conversations from eight major providers (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, Perplexity, Mistral, Qwen, and DeepSeek) — to the general public.

The campaign's core job is twofold: build broad awareness of a problem most people don't realize they have (their AI chat history is fragile and easily lost), and convert that awareness into one-time purchases at the discounted $24 launch price. Since this is TotalRecalls' first major push to market, the campaign also lays the groundwork for word-of-mouth and organic discovery well beyond the initial 60 days.

### Objectives

1. Drive 5,000 unique visits to the TotalRecalls landing page within the first 60 days.
2. Convert at least 8% of free tier users into paid Pro purchases ($24) by day 60.
3. Achieve 1,000 total Pro purchases by the end of the campaign period.
4. Grow combined social following (X, LinkedIn, Facebook) by 2,000 followers within 60 days.
5. Generate at least 500 email sign-ups for ongoing product updates and offers.

### Target Audience

#### Primary audience

General consumers of all ages who regularly use AI assistants like ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini for work, research, creative projects, or everyday questions. Many don't realize how easily their conversation history can be lost — through UI changes, expired sessions, or provider limitations. This audience isn't necessarily technical, but they are AI-curious and value convenience, privacy, and getting more out of the tools they already use. Their pain point is loss aversion: they've likely already lost a valuable chat and felt that sting, or they will soon.

#### Secondary audience

Privacy-conscious professionals and power users (developers, researchers, writers) who use multiple AI providers side-by-side and want a reliable, local way to consolidate and own their data without relying on cloud storage or subscriptions. This group is more likely to be early adopters and vocal advocates if the product delivers on its promise.

### Key Messages

1. **Ownership**: Your AI conversations are yours. TotalRecalls stores them permanently on your own machine — no cloud, no account, no company holding your data hostage.
2. **Loss-aversion & urgency**: You back up your photos and files — why not your AI conversations? They can vanish overnight due to session expirations, UI changes, or provider errors. Protect them now, while you still can.
3. **Convenience**: One simple app covers all eight major AI providers, replacing a patchwork of manual exports and awkward workarounds.
4. **Privacy**: 100% local. Zero cloud upload. Your conversations never leave your machine.
5. **Freedom from subscriptions**: Pay once ($24, 50% off at launch), own it forever. No recurring fees, ever.
6. **Founder authenticity**: Built by someone who personally hit a wall trying to retrieve his own AI conversations — TotalRecalls exists because the problem was real, and the fix didn't.

### Timeline

- **Days 1-5**: Finalize landing page optimizations, prepare all ad creative and copy, set up tracking and analytics.
- **Days 5-14**: Launch search, Meta, X, and LinkedIn ad campaigns. Begin organic social posting cadence. Deploy email welcome sequence for new free tier sign-ups. Publish first two blog posts.
- **Days 14-30**: Continue paid ad optimization based on early performance data. Roll out provider-specific SEO guides on a staggered schedule. Monitor free-to-paid conversion rates and adjust messaging as needed.
- **Day 30**: Launch "Save 50% Before Your Chats Disappear" urgency push across paid and organic social channels.
- **Days 30-45**: Continue publishing provider guides. Send mid-campaign discount reminder email to unconverted free tier users. Analyze which channels and messages are driving the strongest conversions.
- **Day 40**: Feature founder story across social and email to deepen trust and authenticity with the audience.
- **Days 45-60**: Double down on best-performing channels and creative. Begin planning for post-campaign retention and referral strategy.
- **Day 60**: Campaign wrap-up and full performance review against objectives, informing next-phase strategy.
