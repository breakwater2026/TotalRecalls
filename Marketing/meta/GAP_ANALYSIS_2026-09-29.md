## 1. The Plan Fork (P1 — the big one)

**File:** `strategy/TotalRecalls Launch Marketing Plan (Revised) — 2 variants.md`

The two "Revised" plans share only **3 of 47 sentences (6%)**. They are two
generations that diverged, not a base + refinement:

| Dimension | Plan A (9,550 chars) | Plan B (4,173 chars) |
|---|---|---|
| Objectives | 4 — awareness-first funnel | 5 — conversion-first + social proof |
| Key Messages | 4 | 5 (adds explicit Value/pricing) |
| Deliverables | Channel-centric (12 channels, format + role) | Deliverable-centric (with **Owner** column; adds 8× guides, 5-email seq, founder posts) |
| Timeline | **Week 1: explainer live + ads active** (4 weeks total) | **Week 1: teasers + email warm-up**; launch day = Week 2 (5+ weeks) |
| Video strategy | Full section: 5 pillars, funnel mapping, cadence, **5 timestamped scripts** | None (references "short-form video" in overview only) |
| Free tier | Mentioned generically | Specifies "3 providers, 5 conversations/download" |
| Launch email | "Launch sequence" (one line) | "5-email sequence" (named deliverable) |

**The margin is too wide for two documents with the same title.** The
sequencing conflict is material: Plan A has Search/Meta ads live in Week 1;
Plan B has only teasers + warm-up until Week 2. For a $24 launch window,
that's a 7-day difference in revenue capture.

**What to push:** "These are titled identically but fork on objectives,
timeline, and an entire video section. Which is the source of truth? If B is
the later generation, it lost the 5 video scripts and the cadence table. If A
is the later generation, it never got the Owner column or the 5-week ramp.
Reconcile into one plan that keeps: the Owner column, the 5-week structure,
the video scripts + cadence, and the free-tier specifics."

## 2. Email: Plan Says "5-Email Sequence," Reality Is a 4-Email Program (1 Draft + 3 Slots)

**Files:** `email/sequences/Email Sequences 1-3 — Welcome, Upgrade, Urgency.md`,
`email/sequences/Email 1 Welcome — early draft.md`, `email/slot-2/Email 2 — all variants.md`,
`email/slot-3/Email 3 — all variants.md`, `email/slot-4/Email 4 — all variants.md`

Plan B lists "Launch email sequence — 5-email sequence" as a deliverable.
What actually exists:

| Piece | Content | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Welcome email (funnel entry) | Early draft fragment only (`email/sequences/Email 1 Welcome — early draft.md`) | ⚠️ No final version |
| Slot 2 — Free→Pro Upgrade | 6 distinct variants | ✅ Complete (A/B ready) |
| Slot 3 — Launch Urgency | 7 distinct variants | ✅ Complete (A/B ready) |
| Slot 4 — Still on the Fence / Upgrade Offer | 3 distinct variants | ✅ Complete (A/B ready) |

**Hole:** The "5-email sequence" framing doesn't match the 4-email program
that was actually built (Welcome + 3 slots). Sequence 1 (the welcome email
that triggers the entire funnel) is still an early draft — no final, no A/B
variants, no subject-line options. And the plan never named the slot
architecture or the A/B strategy behind slots 2–4.

**What to push:** "The plan promises a 5-email sequence. What we built is a
4-email program: a welcome email (still an early draft) plus 3 slots with
heavy A/B (6, 7, and 3 variants respectively). Either the plan needs to
acknowledge the 4-email architecture (and explain why 5 became 4), or we
need the missing 5th email defined. Separately: the Welcome email is still an
early draft — no final copy, no subject lines, no preheaders. That's the
first email a free-tier user sees. Please produce the final welcome email
with 3 subject-line options."

## 3. Social: Cadence Promised, Program Missing

**Files:** `social/` (4 platforms, 18 post variants), `ops/Launch-Day Social Posting Checklist.md`

Plan A specifies: TikTok 4–5×/week, Reels 3–4×/week, Shorts 3×/week,
long-form bi-weekly. That's **10–14 short-form pieces per week** for a solo
developer. The Missing Items Checklist (1.3) correctly flags "Organic social
program 🟡 Partial — Missing: a weekly cadence across the whole window,
provider-spotlight posts, founder-led series, objection-handling posts."

What exists: **launch-day posts only** (5 Facebook, 4 Instagram, 4 LinkedIn,
5 X/Twitter) + a posting checklist for launch day. No week 2–5 content, no
provider-spotlight series, no founder series, no repurposing rules.

**Hole:** The plan sets an aggressive cadence but the social program to
sustain it doesn't exist. For a solo developer, 10–14 short-form videos/week
may be aspirational rather than operational.

**What to push:** "The plan commits to 10–14 short-form pieces per week. We
have launch-day posts for 4 platforms but nothing for weeks 2–5. Either (a)
build a 4-week social calendar mapping the 18 existing posts + new content to
specific days, or (b) right-size the cadence to what a solo developer can
actually sustain (suggest 3–5×/week total across platforms, with a
repurposing matrix: 1 TikTok → 1 Reel → 1 Short → 1 X thread). We need the
provider-spotlight series (8 guides = 8 posts) and a founder-led series (4–6
posts) defined."

## 4. Ads: Built Ahead of Plan, But No Test Plan

**Files:** `ads/` (5 merged files, 16 ad sections)

All 5 networks have ad copy ready (Google Search + Display + Video, Meta,
LinkedIn, X, generic variations). The Missing Items Checklist marks "Ads:
Not yet created — Pending" which is **stale** — ads are done. (Free-tier
specification gap in the ads: see Section 5.)

**Hole:** Neither plan specifies a **testing strategy** — which headlines to
A/B, which audiences to test first, budget allocation per channel, or
kill/scale thresholds. 16 ad variants exist but no plan says which 2–3 to
launch with, what to measure, or when to swap. The Master Implementation
Plan has a "Testing Roadmap" section but it references the plan without
specifying which ad variants to test.

**What to push:** "We have 16 ad copy variants across 5 networks. The plan
says 'Search: text ads' and 'Meta: static + video' but doesn't say which
variants to launch with, what the test matrix is, or the decision rules
(CTR below X → swap to variant Y). Please add an ad testing plan: for each
network, which 2 variants to launch, primary metric, 7-day review gate, and
the swap/scale/kill thresholds."

## 5. Free Tier Specifics: Plan B Only — and 4 of 5 Ad Networks Silent

Plan B explicitly states "3 providers, 5 conversations/download" as the free
tier. Plan A just says "free tier to try first." Verified across the ad
files: only `ads/generic/` carries the specifics; Google, Meta, X, and
LinkedIn all use vague "Try free" language. Emails and website copy are
consistent and specific. This should be one canonical phrasing everywhere.

**What to push:** "The free tier is '3 providers, 5 conversations per
download.' Plan B and our emails/website copy say this. But 4 of 5 ad
networks (Google, Meta, X, LinkedIn) just say 'Try free' / 'Try the Free
Tier' with no limits. Only the generic ad set carries the specifics. Pick one
canonical phrasing and apply it to the Google, Meta, X, and LinkedIn ad
copy (and keep it consistent in the YouTube scripts). The free tier's limits
are a conversion lever — under-specifying them in the ads sets wrong
expectations and invites support tickets."

## 8. KPIs & Decision Rules: Defined in Plan, Never Operationalized

Both plans reference metrics ("drive awareness," "convert to Pro") but
neither gives numeric targets. The Missing Items Checklist (2.4) flags "KPI
thresholds and decision rules 🟡 Partial — Missing: target CTR by channel,
free-tier download volume, free-to-Pro conversion rate, CAC/CPA ranges, and
'if X then Y' rules."

**What to push:** "For a $24 one-time product with a launch discount window,
we need concrete numbers. Suggested framework: (a) free-tier download target
per week, (b) free-to-Pro conversion rate target (suggest 8–15% for
utility software), (c) CAC ceiling per channel (if CAC > $12, the $24
lifetime value is at risk), (d) ad CTR benchmarks by network (Google Search
>3%, Meta >1.5%, X >0.5%), (e) the 'if X then Y' rules: if CTR < benchmark
after 7 days → swap creative; if free-to-Pro < 5% after 2 weeks → test
pricing page; if CAC > $12 for 7 days → pause channel."
