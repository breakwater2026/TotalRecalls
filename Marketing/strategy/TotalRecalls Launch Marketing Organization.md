2. Consolidated Launch Plan
Product Snapshot
TotalRecalls is a Windows desktop application that downloads a user's AI conversation history — from ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, Grok, DeepSeek, Mistral, and Qwen — into a private local folder as Markdown and JSON files. The product runs 100% locally: no cloud upload, no account required, and no session data transmitted to a server.
Pricing Structure
Free Tier: 3 providers (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity), limited to 5 conversations per download. Designed to let users test the app before buying.
Pro License: One-time purchase of $24 USD (50% off the standard launch price), unlocking all 8 providers, unlimited downloads, and up to 3 device activations. No subscription, ever.
Positioning
TotalRecalls occupies a category of its own: AI conversation backup, distinct from generic note-taking or cloud-sync tools. The core promise is ownership and permanence — conversations saved as plain files on the user's own drive, immune to session expiry, UI overhauls, or provider-side data loss.
Target Audience
Individual power users of AI assistants who:
Rely on AI conversations for ongoing work (research, coding, writing, planning)
Have already experienced — or fear — losing valuable chat history
Value privacy and want a local-first alternative to cloud-based tools
May use multiple AI providers and want one unified archive
Core Messaging Pillars
Loss aversion: Sessions expire, threads get orphaned by UI changes, downloads fail. The live feed on the homepage dramatizes this risk in real time.
Ownership: Files live on the user's own machine in open formats (Markdown + JSON) — no proprietary lock-in, no account to lose.
Simplicity: A four-step process (Buy → Install → Pick providers → Download) makes the value tangible immediately.
Authenticity: The founder story — a retired investment banker turned independent developer — reinforces trust in a market wary of cloud-based AI tools.
Urgency: The 50%-off launch price of $24 is time-limited, creating a clear reason to convert Free Tier users to Pro before the window closes.
Launch Sequencing (High Level)
Weeks 1–2: Announce the launch, tell the founder story, and establish the "AI conversation backup" category.
Weeks 2–6: Roll out provider-specific guide spotlights (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, Grok, DeepSeek, Mistral) to drive organic search traffic and support SEO/AEO visibility for each provider's export process.
Week 6: Apply urgency messaging to convert warm Free Tier users into Pro buyers before the launch discount ends.
Week 6–7: Close out the guide rollout with the final provider (Qwen).
Supporting Assets
Video: The Video Script and Video Outline support both a primary launch video (featuring the Claude demo walkthrough) and a short-form video strategy for social distribution.
Content: The Headlines set feeds into blog titles, ad headlines, and page hero copy.
Guides: The existing 8 provider guides and the "Own your AI chat data" guide serve as the long-form SEO/AEO backbone referenced throughout the content calendar.
Gaps to Close
Ads and email assets have not yet been developed. These should be prioritized to support the Day 1 launch announcement and the Day 42 urgency/promotion push, where paid amplification and direct email conversion will matter most.
