## TotalRecalls Launch Marketing Plan (Revised)


Overview
TotalRecalls is launching as a one-time-purchase Windows app that lets users download and permanently own their AI chat history from eight providers — ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, Grok, DeepSeek, Mistral, and Qwen. This revised plan expands the original channel mix (Search, Meta, X, LinkedIn, Email, Blog, Organic Social) to include YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram, reflecting the addition of a dedicated short-form video strategy to drive awareness, trust, and conversions during the discounted launch window ($24 USD, 50% off).
Objectives
Drive awareness of TotalRecalls among AI power users who don't yet know their chat history is at risk of being lost.
Build trust in a solo-developer product through founder-led storytelling and product transparency.
Convert awareness into Free Tier downloads and, ultimately, Pro purchases at the discounted $24 launch price.
Establish a searchable, evergreen video library (via YouTube/SEO) that continues to generate demand after the launch window closes.
Target Audience
Primary: Frequent users of ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, and other AI chat tools — freelancers, developers, researchers, writers, and knowledge workers who treat AI conversations as work product.
Secondary: Privacy-conscious users wary of cloud storage and data lock-in.
Platform note: TotalRecalls is Windows-only today. Mobile-first platforms (TikTok, Instagram) require a CTA that clearly directs viewers to visit the website from a desktop or save the link for later — never implying an in-app or mobile download.
Key Messages
Loss aversion — "You back up your files. Why not your AI conversations?"
Urgency — "Protect your conversations while you can" (tied to the limited-time 50%-off launch price).
Provider friction — "Trying to get your conversations back from ChatGPT or Claude? Good luck."
Founder authenticity — "This product came from my own inability to get my AI conversations back from the big providers."

## TotalRecalls Launch Marketing Plan (Revised) (alt)


Overview
TotalRecalls is a Windows desktop app that lets users download their entire AI chat history — from ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, Grok, DeepSeek, Mistral, and Qwen — into a private, local folder as Markdown and JSON files. No cloud, no subscription, no account. This revised plan expands the original channel mix (Search, Meta, X, LinkedIn, Email, Blog, Organic Social) to formally include YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram, reflecting the growing role of short-form video in driving both awareness and trust for the launch.
Objectives
Drive Pro license purchases at the discounted launch price of $24 USD (one-time, no subscription)
Build category awareness around the risk of losing AI conversation history
Establish TotalRecalls as the trustworthy, privacy-first solution among AI power users
Convert free-tier trials (3 providers, 5 conversations/download) into Pro upgrades
Generate founder-led social proof to build early credibility
Target Audience
Knowledge workers, developers, researchers, and writers who rely on multiple AI assistants daily
Privacy-conscious users wary of cloud storage and vendor lock-in
Power users who have already lost a valuable conversation to a UI change, session expiry, or platform migration
Windows 10/11 users (primary); note that mobile platform audiences must be guided to desktop for install
Key Messages
Loss aversion: "You back up your files. Why not your AI conversations?"
Urgency: "Protect your conversations while you can."
Provider friction: "Trying to get your conversations back from AI providers? Good luck."
Founder authenticity: "This product came from my own inability to get my AI conversations back from the big providers."
Value/pricing: One-time $24 USD launch price (50% off), no subscription, lifetime license, free tier available to try first.
