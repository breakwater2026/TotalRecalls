How to use this checklist
Start with the P1 rollup. These block launch day — clear them before touching anything lower.
Turn ⬜ and 🟡 items into scheduled tasks with an owner and a date.
Re-check statuses daily during the launch window and move items to ✅ as you close them.
Treat infrastructure readiness as a gate. If any of its items is unchecked, hold the launch.
Once every P1 shows ✅ and the remaining items are scheduled, your launch shifts from a folder of content to a plan you can actually run.
