# Short-Form Video Strategy

Content Pillars
Loss Aversion — You already back up your files; your AI conversations deserve the same protection.
Provider Friction — AI providers make it hard (or impossible) to reliably get your own conversations back.
Founder Authenticity — This product exists because the founder lived the problem himself.
Urgency — Every day without a backup is a day you risk losing months of work.
Proof & Education — Real demos showing exactly how the app works, provider by provider.

Video Scripts
1. Main Product Explainer (45–90 sec)
Platforms: YouTube, Instagram Reels, Meta
Goal: Educate and convert — show the full value proposition.
[0:00–0:05] Hook
Visual: Fast montage of chat windows glitching, sessions expiring, "thread not found" errors.
VO: "Every day, people lose months of AI conversations — and never get them back."
[0:06–0:15] Problem
Visual: Screen recording of trying to export a ChatGPT thread, hitting a dead end.
VO: "You back up your photos. Your files. Even your phone. But your AI conversations? Gone the second the tab closes or the provider changes their UI."
[0:16–0:35] Solution Reveal
Visual: TotalRecalls app opens on screen; provider icons (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, Grok, DeepSeek, Mistral, Qwen) light up one by one.
VO: "Meet TotalRecalls — a Windows app that downloads your conversations from all eight major AI providers into one private folder on your own computer. No cloud. No account. Just your chats, saved as Markdown and JSON, forever."
[0:36–0:55] Demo
Visual: Screen recording — select provider, sign in, choose folder, watch files populate in File Explorer.
VO: "Pick a provider, sign in, choose where to save — and watch your entire chat history land on your drive in minutes."
[0:56–1:10] Trust/Proof
Visual: Text overlay: "100% local. Zero cloud upload." Folder icon with lock.
VO: "Everything stays on your machine. Nothing is ever uploaded anywhere."
[1:11–1:25] Offer + CTA
Visual: Pricing card: "$24 USD — one-time, 50% off launch price. No subscription."
VO: "Try it free right now, then unlock every provider and unlimited downloads for a one-time $24. No subscription, ever."
Text overlay: "Download free on totalrecalls.app — Windows 10/11"
