## Review notes

### A. Things the original script says that the video does not show
1. **Stage 6 "Instant Search & Query Mastery" does not exist in the video.** There is no search bar or query anywhere in 0:00-4:37. If the app has search, it needs a new recording. Otherwise, remove the search claims from the narration (including "advanced local search engine" and "in milliseconds").
2. **"Extract the ZIP" never happens.** The exe is run from inside the ZIP, which triggers an extra "Compressed (zipped) Folders" warning. The website says "Just unzip and run", so extracting first would match both the site and the original narration.
3. **"All 8 providers fully unlocked" is never shown.** After activation the dropdown is not reopened. A 5-second re-record (open the dropdown after the PRO badge appears) would prove it. "8+" in the original should say "8".
4. **"Local indexing" is not shown.** The video shows downloading and saving files, with no indexing step.
5. The original script does not mention several real steps: the ChatGPT login, saving the free output and checking it in Explorer, the license-key entry, the "Re-download everything" tick, the 4x speed badge, and the file-conversion dialog.

### B. Narration or claim risks
6. **"Blazing-fast" over a 4x-speed section.** The Pro download is labelled 4x, and the full run covers 1:54 of video (about 7.5 minutes real time). I kept the "shown at four times speed" disclosure in the narration. Please check this against your marketing claims.
7. **SmartScreen wording.** The original said "100% safe" and "will disappear as we scale". I softened it. The site itself says code signing is on the roadmap, and that Windows 11 Smart App Control can block unsigned apps with no override, so some viewers will not get a "Run anyway" button at all.
8. **Narration length does not fit stages 1-2.** The original opener was about 65 words (about 26 s) over a 15 s portion of video. I trimmed it across Stages 1-3.

### C. Recording issues worth fixing
9. **Duplicate download.** The Downloads panel shows two ZIPs and the one opened is `...(1).zip`. This looks like a retake.
10. **Markdown and JSON open in Microsoft Word**, with an encoding dialog and no formatting. For "clean, beautiful Markdown" use a Markdown viewer or editor (Notepad++ appears in the Explorer sidebar). For JSON use an editor with syntax highlighting.
11. **Layout.** The recording is a 3440x1440 ultrawide. The left third is an empty Word window for almost the whole video, and the app window is small on screen, so text is hard to read at normal video sizes. Crop or zoom in on the active window.
12. **Privacy.** Explorer's date column is blurred but conversation titles are still partly readable in the folder list. In Stage 13/14 the Word view shows real conversation content, including personal trading details written by the user. Blur it, or use a demo account.
13. **License key on screen** (`TR-DEMO0000-0001-0001`). Confirm it is a demo key that cannot be redeemed by viewers.
14. **Stage 11 pacing.** Nearly two minutes with little cursor activity. Consider speeding this up further (for example 16x) or cutting to a few key moments (start, midpoint, "106/106").
15. **Unused features visible on screen.** "Export part of a conversation" (select individual messages or turns), "Copy latest Markdown" and "Save latest as PDF" are visible but not demonstrated.
