	Gemini Takeout Troubleshooting
	Gemini exports your conversations through Google Takeout.

	1. Use the correct Takeout option

	Takeout/Apps/Gemini/

	Do not point TotalRecalls to the ZIP itself.
	Windows must extract the files first.

	If you have several Takeout downloads, use the newest one.

	Let TotalRecalls finish scanning the folder.
