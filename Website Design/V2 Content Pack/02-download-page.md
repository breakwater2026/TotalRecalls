## How to use the app (4 steps)

1. **Open TotalRecalls.exe** — No installation required.
2. **Pick a provider** — ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini (Takeout), or Grok.
3. **Sign in locally** — A small window opens so you can sign in normally — just like a browser. (Gemini uses your Google Takeout folder.)
4. **Export** — Your chats land in a folder you choose, under Library/, as Markdown + JSON.
