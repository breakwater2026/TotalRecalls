## Why one-time pricing?

TotalRecalls is a utility, not a subscription service. You pay once, download the app, and keep your archive forever.

Later, when advanced features arrive (semantic search, Obsidian/Notion sync, RAG containers, multi-device memory), those will be **optional upgrades** — not required for basic exporting.

Today's launch price is for the core export tool.
