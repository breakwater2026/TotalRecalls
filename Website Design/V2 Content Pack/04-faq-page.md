## Q&A items (verbatim conclusions)

**What is TotalRecalls?**
A small Windows app that saves your ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, and Grok conversations into a private folder on your computer — readable Markdown + JSON you keep forever.

**How much does it cost?**
~~$49~~ $24 launch price. One-time purchase. No subscription. No monthly "AI archive" fee.

**Do I need an account?**
No. TotalRecalls does not require a TotalRecalls account. You sign in only to the AI providers you already use.

**Do you store my chats or tokens?**
No. Your AI session tokens stay on your machine. We never host your chats, your exports, or your credentials.

**Does TotalRecalls run in the cloud?**
No. It's a local Windows app. Exports land in a folder you choose on your PC.

**What providers are supported?**
ChatGPT · Claude · Perplexity · Gemini (via Google Takeout or local sign-in) · Grok. Version: v1.3.0.

**How do exports work?**
1. Open the app
2. Pick a provider
3. Sign in locally (or choose a Gemini Takeout folder)
4. Export — your chats appear as Markdown + JSON under a Library/ folder

**Does this replace official exports?**
No — official exports are fine for compliance dumps. TotalRecalls is for a usable personal archive you can browse, search, and back up.

**Is the app safe?**
Yes. But because the build is not yet code-signed, Windows may show a SmartScreen warning: "Windows protected your PC". Click More info → Run anyway if you trust this download from totalrecalls.app. Smart App Control on Surface devices may block unsigned apps with no override. Code-signing is on the roadmap.

**Does it work on Mac?**
Not yet. Mac support will be added if demand is real.

**Does it work on mobile?**
No. Windows only.

**What's inside the ZIP?**
TotalRecalls.exe · README.txt · LICENSE.txt. No installers. No background services. No telemetry.

**Will future features cost extra?**
The $24 launch price covers the core export tool. Advanced features (semantic search, Obsidian/Notion sync, RAG containers, multi-device memory) may be optional upgrades later.

**Can I get help?**
Yes — see the guides: ChatGPT export, Claude Projects, Perplexity threads, Gemini Takeout, Multi-assistant workflows. Visit Help & Guides.
