**Subject:** Thanks for being early — here's what's next

Thanks for picking up TotalRecalls early. You now own a tool that gives you permanent copies of your AI conversations — across ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, and Grok — saved locally as Markdown + JSON.

**Mid-term**
- Semantic search across your archive
- Obsidian + Notion sync
- Multi-assistant timeline view
- Multi-device memory (optional upgrade)

**Long-term**
- RAG containers
- Personal memory engine
- Cross-assistant recall
- Optional cloud sync (opt-in)

You paid once. You own the current tool forever. Future upgrades will be optional — never required.

If you hit any issues or have feature requests, reply anytime.

Thanks again for supporting the launch.

— TotalRecalls
