## Why this matters

AI assistants are becoming the place where your thinking happens — research, planning, writing, debugging, learning, and long threads of reasoning. But none of them give you real ownership.

- ChatGPT stores everything in the cloud
- Claude's Projects live behind an account
- Perplexity deletes history after 30 days
- Gemini requires Google Takeout
- Grok keeps everything online

Your work is scattered across five companies, five UIs, and five histories that can change at any time.

TotalRecalls gives you a single, durable archive you control.

A folder of Markdown on your disk still opens in ten years. It survives account changes, UI redesigns, pricing shifts, and platform churn.

**Your conversations become files — not tabs.**

This is why TotalRecalls exists.
