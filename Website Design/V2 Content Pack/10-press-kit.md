**Product Name:** TotalRecalls — Own every AI conversation

**Tagline:**
A small Windows app that saves your ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, and Grok chats into a private folder on your PC — Markdown + JSON you keep forever.

**Short Description:**
TotalRecalls exports your AI conversations into files you own. No subscription. No cloud locker. No account required.

**Long Description:**
TotalRecalls is a Windows utility that creates a durable, local archive of your AI conversations. It exports chats from ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini (Takeout), and Grok into a clean folder structure on your PC — readable Markdown + JSON you can browse, search, and back up.

Your chats stay on your disk. We never host your session tokens. Sign-in happens locally.

**Key Features:**

- Local Markdown + JSON archive
- Private folder structure
- No cloud storage
- No telemetry
- No subscription
- Windows 10/11
