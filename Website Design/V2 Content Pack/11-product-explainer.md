## What is TotalRecalls?

AI assistants are becoming the place where your thinking happens — research, planning, writing, debugging, learning, and long threads of reasoning. But none of them give you real ownership.

TotalRecalls is a small Windows app that fixes that.

It exports your ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, and Grok conversations into a private folder on your computer — readable Markdown + JSON you keep forever.

- Your chats become files, not tabs.
- Your archive becomes durable, not fragile.
- Your work becomes yours, not theirs.

One payment. No subscription. Your conversations stay on your disk.
