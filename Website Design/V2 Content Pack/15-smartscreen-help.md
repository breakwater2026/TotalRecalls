## Windows SmartScreen — Help

Because TotalRecalls is not yet code-signed, Windows may show:

> "Windows protected your PC"

This is normal for new apps.

### How to proceed
1. Click **More info**
2. Click **Run anyway**
3. TotalRecalls will open normally

### Why this happens
SmartScreen warns about apps that are new or unsigned. TotalRecalls is safe to run if you downloaded it from totalrecalls.app.

### Surface devices (Smart App Control)
Some Surface models may block unsigned apps with no override. Code-signing is on the roadmap.

### We never host your chats
TotalRecalls runs locally. Your AI session tokens stay on your machine.
