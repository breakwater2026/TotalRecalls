## TotalRecalls — Own Every AI Conversation

### Overview
AI assistants are where your thinking happens — research, planning, writing, debugging, learning. But none of them give you real ownership. Your conversations live behind accounts, tabs, and cloud histories that can change at any time.

TotalRecalls fixes this. It's a small Windows app that exports your ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, and Grok conversations into a private folder on your computer — readable Markdown + JSON you keep forever.

Your chats. Your disk. One-time purchase.

### Key Features

**Multi-assistant export** — ChatGPT · Claude · Perplexity · Gemini (Takeout) · Grok. All in one place.

**Local archive** — Your conversations become files — not tabs. Markdown + JSON you can browse, search, and back up.

**Private by design** — No cloud locker · No telemetry · No TotalRecalls account · Session tokens stay on your machine

**Simple commercial model** — ~~$49~~ $24 launch price. One-time purchase. No subscription.

### Why It Matters
AI apps change. Tabs close. Histories disappear. A folder of Markdown on your PC still opens in ten years.

TotalRecalls gives you: durability · portability · ownership · independence from provider UIs · a single library across assistants.

Your work becomes yours, not theirs.

### How It Works
1. Buy once — checkout via Lemon Squeezy
2. Download the Windows ZIP
3. Pick a provider
4. Sign in locally
5. Export — your chats land under Library/ as Markdown + JSON
