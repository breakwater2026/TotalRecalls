## Feature Requests

We're building TotalRecalls for people who want durable, local ownership of their AI conversations. If you have ideas, improvements, or workflows you want supported, we'd love to hear them.

### What to send

1. **Provider requests** — New assistants you want supported.
2. **Export improvements** — Formatting, metadata, folder structure, speed.
3. **Workflow enhancements** — Search, tagging, merging, filtering, timeline views.
4. **Integrations** — Obsidian, Notion, Logseq, VS Code, local RAG tools.
5. **Advanced features** — Semantic search, multi-device memory, RAG containers.
6. **Bugs** — Anything broken, inconsistent, or unclear.

### How to submit

**Email:** support@totalrecalls.app
**GitHub Issues:** github.com/breakwater2026/TotalRecalls/issues

In your message, include:
- Provider (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, Grok)
- What you expected
- What happened
- Screenshots (optional)
- Export folder samples (optional)

### Our principles
Local-first · No forced subscription · Durable archives · Multi-assistant workflows · Clear, predictable exports · Optional upgrades only

### Thank you
Early feedback shapes the roadmap. Your ideas help make TotalRecalls better for everyone.
