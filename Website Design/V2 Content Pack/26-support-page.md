## Support

We're here to help you own your AI conversations — cleanly, privately, and without friction.

### Common Topics
- SmartScreen warnings
- Gemini Takeout issues
- Provider login problems
- Missing conversations
- Export speed
- Folder permissions
- Antivirus false positives

### Contact Support
Email: support@totalrecalls.app

Include:
- Provider (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, Grok)
- What you expected
- What happened
- Screenshots (optional)
- Export folder samples (optional)

### Refunds
If TotalRecalls doesn't work for your setup, email support within 14 days for a refund.

### Feature Requests
We welcome ideas. See the Feature Request page for details.

### Known Limitations
- Windows only
- Unsigned builds may trigger SmartScreen
- Surface Smart App Control may block unsigned apps
- Some providers rate-limit long exports
- Gemini Takeout formats vary

### Guides
Visit the Guides page for step-by-step instructions.
