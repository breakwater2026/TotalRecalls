## Privacy Policy

TotalRecalls is built on a simple principle: **Your conversations belong to you.**

We do not collect, store, or transmit your AI chats, session tokens, or export data.

### What TotalRecalls stores
Nothing.

TotalRecalls does not:
- store your chats
- store your session tokens
- store your passwords
- store your exports
- store analytics
- store telemetry
- create a cloud account
- send your data to our servers

Everything stays on your PC.

### Local-only design
TotalRecalls runs locally. Sign-in happens inside a small embedded browser window on your machine. We never see your credentials.

### Session tokens
Your AI provider session tokens remain on your device. They are not transmitted to TotalRecalls servers.

### Export data
Your exported conversations land in a folder you choose. We do not have access to this folder.

### Crash logs
TotalRecalls does not automatically send crash logs. If you want help diagnosing an issue, you may send logs manually.

### Third-party services
TotalRecalls uses:
- Lemon Squeezy for payments
- Microsoft WebView2 for embedded sign-in windows

Both operate independently of TotalRecalls.
