## Obsidian & Notion Integration — Preview

TotalRecalls will soon integrate with Obsidian and Notion, allowing you to turn your AI conversations into durable knowledge you can search, link, and build on.

This page previews what's coming.

### Obsidian Integration

**What it will do:**
- Sync your Library folder into an Obsidian vault
- Preserve Markdown formatting
- Add metadata frontmatter
- Enable backlinks between conversations
- Support tags and folders
- Optional auto-sync

**Why it matters:**
Obsidian is the best place to build long-term personal knowledge. Your AI conversations become part of your thinking system.

### Notion Integration

**What it will do:**
- Import conversations into a Notion database
- Add metadata fields (provider, date, tags)
- Support full-text search
- Enable filtering and sorting
- Optional auto-sync

**Why it matters:**
Notion is ideal for structured research, project tracking, and team workflows.

### Timeline
- Obsidian: early preview
- Notion: early preview
- Sync engine: in development
- Optional paid upgrade: likely

### Principles
Local-first · No forced cloud sync · Optional upgrades only · Durable knowledge
