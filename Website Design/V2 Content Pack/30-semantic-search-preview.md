## Semantic Search — Preview

Semantic search will let you search across all your AI conversations — regardless of provider — using meaning, not keywords.

### What semantic search will do
- Search across ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, and Grok
- Understand meaning, not just text
- Group related conversations
- Surface forgotten threads
- Provide "memory-like" recall
- Enable cross-assistant insights

### Why it matters
Your AI conversations contain: research · planning · debugging · writing · learning · long reasoning threads.

Semantic search turns your archive into a personal knowledge engine.

### How it will work
- Local embedding generation
- Local vector store
- Local semantic search
- Optional cloud sync (opt-in)
- Optional paid upgrade

### Timeline
- Prototype in development
- Preview planned
- Release after code-signed builds

### Principles
Local-first · Private · Durable · Optional upgrades only
