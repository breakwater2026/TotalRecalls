Pictory AI handles movement in images through several methods, though it has some limitations compared to dedicated animation software. Here's how it works:

## Image Movement in Pictory AI

### Built-in Motion Effects

1. **Ken Burns Effect**: 
   - Pictory automatically applies subtle pan and zoom effects to static images
   - You can customize the direction (pan left, right, zoom in, zoom out)
   - This creates a sense of movement even with completely static Leonardo AI images

2. **Transition Animations**:
   - Slide transitions create movement between scenes
   - Fade, dissolve, wipe, and other transitions add dynamic flow
   - These work well to connect your Leonardo AI generated scenes

3. **Text Animations**:
   - Text can enter/exit the frame with motion
   - Captions can be animated independently from background images
   - Useful for song lyrics or emphasizing key words

### Video Clips vs. Static Images

For more complex movement, Pictory handles different media types:

1. **Static Images (from Leonardo AI)**:
   - Limited to Ken Burns-style movement effects
   - Can be layered to create parallax effects (foreground/background moving at different speeds)
   - Works well for scene backgrounds and non-speaking elements

2. **Video Clips**:
   - You can upload short video clips that already contain movement
   - These play as normal videos within your Pictory project
   - Useful for integrating your lip-synced character animations

### Limitations and Workarounds

Pictory AI isn't designed for complex animation, so you'll need to work within these constraints:

1. **Limited Layer Control**:
   - Pictory doesn't offer true multi-layer animation
   - For character movement, pre-animate in other software and import as video clips

2. **No Frame-by-Frame Animation**:
   - You cannot animate individual elements within an image in Pictory itself
   - Create any character movements in your lip-sync software first

3. **Movement Duration Control**:
   - You can adjust how long each scene displays
   - This affects the speed of any Ken Burns effects
   - Shorter scenes = faster movements

1. **Prepare Layered Images in Leonardo AI**:
