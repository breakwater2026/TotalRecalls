This is a beautiful, personal note. Here are the corrections and suggestions:

**Paragraph 2 — Awkward construction and wrong preposition:**
"Reminiscing on my way back about that study group session, where, of course, you cracked the case for us, he left me an impression of a devoted man deeply in love **for** you and his family."

→ *"On my way back, reminiscing about that study group session where you, of course, cracked the case for us, I was left with the impression of a devoted man deeply in love **with** you and his family."*

The original has a dangling modifier (it reads as though *he* was reminiscing), and "in love for" should be "in love with."

**Paragraph 4 — Awkward phrasing:**
"He only had praises comment for Stu"

→ *"He had nothing but praise for Stu"*

**One small optional tweak** in paragraph 1: "he left me an impression" in the original (before the Reminiscing sentence) could simply be cut, as the revised sentence below it captures that thought more elegantly.

Everything else reads well — the grammar is solid, the tone is warm and consistent, and the personal details make it feel genuinely yours. The line about Stu presenting "well-behaved Amy, Josh and little Ben who was all over the place" is particularly lovely — keep that exactly as is.
