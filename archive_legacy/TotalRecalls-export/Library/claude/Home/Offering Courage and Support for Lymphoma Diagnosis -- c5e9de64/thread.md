I'm very sorry to hear about your son's lymphoma diagnosis. This must be an incredibly difficult and emotional time for your entire family.

Here is a message you could share with your son and his fiancée:

"We want you both to know that you are not alone in facing this challenge. While we know this diagnosis brings uncertainty and fear, we also know the incredible strength and determination you possess. Your bond as a couple is inspiring, and together with the support of your medical team and family, you have a powerful foundation for this journey ahead.

Remember that it's okay to have difficult days and to lean on those around you. We will be here every step of the way - whether you need practical support, a listening ear, or simply a moment of normalcy and distraction. Modern medicine has made remarkable advances in treating lymphoma, and many people go on to live full, healthy lives after treatment.

Your courage in facing this challenge head-on moves us deeply. Take each day as it comes, celebrate small victories, and know that you are surrounded by endless love and support. We have absolute faith in your resilience and strength."

Please know that as parents, you may also need support during this time. Consider reaching out to cancer support groups for families, as they can provide valuable resources and connection with others who understand what you're going through.
