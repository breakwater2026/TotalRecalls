*Each episode follows a standard 8-minute format set in "Friendship Park"*

## Episode 1: "The New Friend"
*Theme: Inclusivity & Kindness*

### Setting
A sunny day at the playground with colorful play equipment and a sandbox.

### Main Characters
- Maya: An outgoing 7-year-old girl who loves to organize games
- Li: A shy new child who recently moved to the neighborhood
- Sam: Maya's best friend who initially feels threatened by Li

### Story Breakdown (8 minutes)
**Minutes 0-2:**
- Maya sees Li standing alone by the swings
- Li is holding a unique toy from their home country
- Other children are playing but haven't noticed Li

**Minutes 2-4:**
- Maya approaches Li but notices Sam looking upset
- Sam worries about losing their best friend
- Li shares interesting stories about their former home

**Minutes 4-6:**
- Maya organizes a group game incorporating Li's toy
- Sam realizes having a new friend doesn't mean losing old ones
- Children learn about Li's culture through play

**Minutes 6-8:**
- The group creates a new game combining their different play styles
- Everyone learns to say "friend" in Li's language
- The episode ends with the three children making plans for tomorrow

### Learning Moments
- How to approach someone new
- Dealing with friendship jealousy
- Celebrating cultural differences
- Creating inclusive games

The ".md" extension stands for "Markdown." Markdown is a lightweight markup language created by John Gruber and Aaron Swartz in 2004. It's designed to be easy to read and write in plain text format, but can be converted into HTML or other formats.

Some key points about Markdown (.md):
1. It's widely used for documentation and README files, especially on platforms like GitHub
2. It uses simple symbols for formatting:
   - `#` for headers
   - `*` or `_` for italics
   - `**` or `__` for bold
   - `-` or `*` for bullet points
   - Numbers for ordered lists
   - `>` for blockquotes

However, for your wall art designs, you'll actually want to save them with the `.svg` extension since they are SVG (Scalable Vector Graphics) files. SVG is the ideal format for:
- Vector graphics that can be scaled without losing quality
- Print materials
- Designs that need to be edited later
- Maintaining exact font specifications and positioning

So when you download these designs, make sure to save them with the `.svg` extension to preserve all the vector properties and ensure the highest quality for printing.
