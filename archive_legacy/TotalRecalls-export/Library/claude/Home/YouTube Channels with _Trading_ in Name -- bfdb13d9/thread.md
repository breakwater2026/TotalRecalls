Let me explain alliteration in more detail. 

Alliteration is when multiple words in a phrase begin with the same consonant sound. In "The Thoughtful Trader," both main words begin with the "Th" sound. 

Our brains are naturally drawn to patterns, and alliteration creates a pleasing sound pattern that makes phrases more memorable. Think of famous brands and characters that use this technique:

- Coca-Cola
- PayPal
- Best Buy
- Mickey Mouse
- Donald Duck

When someone is scrolling through YouTube and sees multiple trading channels, the alliterative pattern of "Thoughtful Trader" might make it stick in their mind more easily than "Teaching Trader," which doesn't have this sound repetition.
