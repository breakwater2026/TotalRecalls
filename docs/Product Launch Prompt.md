It supports eight providers: ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, Grok, DeepSeek, Mistral, and Qwen. Conversations are stored locally in plain Markdown and JSON. No cloud intermediary is used, and the application does not host or transmit session tokens.

TotalRecalls is developed and maintained by a sole proprietor. It is sold under a one‑time perpetual license (no subscription). The free tier allows limited downloads from three providers (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity) and up to five conversations. The Pro tier unlocks all eight providers, unlimited downloads, and three device activations for a one‑time launch price of $24 USD.
