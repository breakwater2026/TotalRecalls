## 1. What is Smart Search Engine (SSE)?

When you use ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, or other AI assistants, your work quickly scatters into hundreds of chats across different websites. 

* **TotalRecalls Downloader** saves those conversations to your computer as clean Markdown and JSON files.
* **Smart Search Engine (SSE)** is the companion search add-on that lets you search and chat with your entire archive in plain English—completely offline, right on your PC.

Nothing is uploaded to the cloud. Your data stays entirely in your hands.

## 3. How to Launch & Activate (4 Steps)

### Step 1: Extract the Files
Unzip the downloaded `TotalRecalls-Smart-Search-Engine-1.0.0.zip` archive into any folder on your PC (for example, in your Documents folder or on your Desktop).

### Step 2: Open the Search Engine
Double-click:  
👉 **`run-smart-search.bat`**  
*(This opens the Smart Search Engine chat interface directly in Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome).*

### Step 3: Activate Your License
1. In the top-right corner, click **Activate SSE Key**.
2. Paste your `SSE-XXXXXXXX-XXXX-XXXX` license key into the box.
3. Click **Activate SSE**.
4. The badge will switch to **SSE Licensed**.  
   *(Activation verifies your key online once; after that, all search, vector indexing, and questions run 100% offline on your PC).*

### Step 4: Connect Your Chat Library
This is the step that puts *your* conversations into the app. Without it you only see five sample chats that ship with the program.

1. Click **Connect Library folder…** in the banner at the top.
2. Pick your library folder — normally:  
   `C:\Users\<YourUsername>\TotalRecalls-download\Library\`  
   *(Choose the `Library` folder itself — the one with `chatgpt`, `claude`, `gemini`… inside it.)*
3. Wait a few seconds. The banner turns green and shows your own numbers, e.g. *464 conversations · 9,317 messages · 8 providers*.
4. That's it. Your index is saved on your PC, so next time you open the app your chats are already there. After a fresh download, click **Re-scan folder…**.

> **Told you so:** if the banner still says *Demo mode*, your licence is not active yet — go back to Step 3. Connecting a library needs an activated copy.

## 4. Three Ways to Search Your Chats

### 1. By Meaning (Semantic Search)
You don’t need to remember the exact keywords you used. Ask natural questions like:  
* *"How did I configure automated database backups?"*  
* *"What did Claude recommend for fixing that Python bug?"*  
SSE finds the most relevant passages based on the concept and meaning.

### 2. By Exact Keyword (Lexical Search)
Use this when you remember a specific term, code snippet, model name, error code, or person’s name.

### 3. Hybrid Search (Best Overall)
Blends meaning and exact keywords together so you get both conceptual accuracy and exact match precision.

## 5. What You See in Your Search Results

Every result includes:
* **The AI Provider & Date:** (e.g., ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity).
* **The Highlighted Answer:** The exact passage answering your question.
* **Conversation Title:** Which of your chats the passage came from — open it in **Chat Explorer** to read the whole conversation.
* **Match Score:** How closely the passage matches your query.

> **Note:** results point at the *conversation*, not at a file you can double-click. To read the raw export, open the conversation folder in your Library from Windows Explorer.

## 7. If Something Looks Wrong

**"It says 5 chats — where are mine?"**
You have not connected your library yet. See Step 4. Five is the sample set that ships with the app.

**"There is no Connect button, only Activate SSE key."**
Your copy is unlicensed. Activation is what unlocks library importing; the demo is deliberately limited to the five samples.

**"I downloaded new conversations but the app doesn't show them."**
Click **Re-scan folder…** in the green banner. The index is a snapshot, not a live watch on the folder.

**"Activation says it could not reach the server."**
Activation needs internet exactly once. Check the connection, then try again. If it is already activated, you can work offline indefinitely.

**"I reinstalled / moved the folder."**
Open the app, click **Re-scan folder…** and pick the Library again. Your chats are never deleted by the app.

**"It says this key has reached its 3-device limit."**
Open the app on a machine you no longer use and click **Deactivate (free slot)**, then activate here.
