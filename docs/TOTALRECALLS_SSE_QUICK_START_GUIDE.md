## 1. What is Smart Search Engine (SSE)?

When you use ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, or other AI assistants, your work quickly scatters into hundreds of chats across different websites. 

* **TotalRecalls Downloader** saves those conversations to your computer as clean Markdown and JSON files.
* **Smart Search Engine (SSE)** is the companion search add-on that lets you search and chat with your entire archive in plain English—completely offline, right on your PC.

Nothing is uploaded to the cloud. Your data stays entirely in your hands.

1. **TotalRecalls Downloader Pro:**

## 3. How to Launch & Activate (3 Steps)

### Step 1: Extract the Files
Unzip the downloaded `TotalRecalls-Smart-Search-Engine-1.0.0.zip` archive into any folder on your PC (for example, in your Documents folder or on your Desktop).

### Step 2: Open the Search Engine
Double-click:  
👉 **`run-smart-search.bat`**  
*(This opens the Smart Search Engine chat interface directly in Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome).*

### Step 3: Activate Your License
1. In the top-right corner, click **Activate SSE Key**.
2. Paste your `SSE-XXXXXXXX-XXXX-XXXX` license key into the box.
3. Click **Activate SSE**.
4. The badge will switch to **SSE Licensed**.  
   *(Activation verifies your key online once; after that, all search, vector indexing, and questions run 100% offline on your PC).*

## 4. Three Ways to Search Your Chats

### 1. By Meaning (Semantic Search)
You don’t need to remember the exact keywords you used. Ask natural questions like:  
* *"How did I configure automated database backups?"*  
* *"What did Claude recommend for fixing that Python bug?"*  
SSE finds the most relevant passages based on the concept and meaning.

### 2. By Exact Keyword (Lexical Search)
Use this when you remember a specific term, code snippet, model name, error code, or person’s name.

### 3. Hybrid Search (Best Overall)
Blends meaning and exact keywords together so you get both conceptual accuracy and exact match precision.

## 5. What You See in Your Search Results

Every result includes:
* **The AI Provider & Date:** (e.g., ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, Mistral, DeepSeek, Grok, Qwen).
* **The Highlighted Answer:** The exact paragraph answering your question.
* **Match Score:** How closely the answer matches your query.
* **Direct File Link:** Click to open the original Markdown (.md) or JSON (.json) file directly on your computer in Notepad, Word, or VS Code.
