## The 4 Ways to Search Made Simple

You don't need to remember exact words or phrases. TotalRecalls offers four ways to find what you're looking for:

### 1. Idea Match (Semantic Vector Search)

**Finding answers when you don't recall the exact words used.**

Example: *"What were those tips on fixing our air conditioner last summer?"*

Even if you don't remember saying "air conditioner" or "summer," TotalRecalls understands the *idea* behind your question and finds conversations about cooling, HVAC, or home repair.

### 2. Numbers & Dates (RAG SQL)

**Instant counts and analytics without doing math.**

Example: *"How many chats did I have with Claude last month?"*

TotalRecalls can count conversations, add up token usage, compare providers, and give you structured answers—no spreadsheets required.

### 3. Exact Words + Ideas (Hybrid RRF)

**The best of both worlds.**

This combines Idea Match with exact keyword matching. It's perfect when you're looking for something specific like an error code (e.g., `ECONNREFUSED`) or a function name, but you also want conceptually similar results.

### 4. AI Peer Review (Consensus Auditor)

**Compare conflicting answers from different AIs.**

Example: You asked ChatGPT and Claude about a legal or medical question, and they gave different advice. The Consensus Auditor shows you where they agree and where they disagree, so you can make a better decision.
