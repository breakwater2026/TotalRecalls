TotalRecalls Summary Business Plan

1.Product
TotalRecalls is a Windows desktop application that downloads users’ AI chat histories directly into a private folder on their computer.
With one click, users can export conversations from eight providers into a single, organized, and searchable local library:
* ChatGPT
* Claude
* Perplexity
* Gemini
* Grok
* DeepSeek
* Mistral
* Qwen
There is no cloud intermediary. Conversations are stored locally in plain, readable Markdown and JSON formats. TotalRecalls does not host or transmit users’ session tokens.
* Website: https://totalrecalls.app  (ready to be deployed. Awaiting our LS account approval)
* Contact: support@totalrecall.app
* Press: press@totalrecalls.app

2. Pricing
Free tier
TotalRecalls is available as a free download. The free tier provides access to three providers—Perplexity, ChatGPT, and Claude—and allows users to download up to five conversations.
This enables users to evaluate the application before purchasing.
Pro license
The Pro version is available for a one-time discounted launch price of $24 USD. There is no subscription and no recurring billing.
The purchase provides:
* Access to all eight supported providers.
* Unlimited conversation downloads.
* Up to three device activations.
* A license key delivered by email through Lemon Squeezy.
* Free updates to the purchased application.

5. Product and sales model
What we sell
TotalRecalls is a Windows desktop application that helps users permanently retain and control their AI conversations. It downloads a user’s own chat history from eight AI providers into local Markdown and JSON files that the user owns and controls.
Development and licensing
TotalRecalls is developed and maintained in-house by me as a sole proprietor. It is a closed-source Windows application sold under a one-time, perpetual digital license.
The customer downloads the locked limited-features free-tier application from the TotalRecalls website. That website contains a Lemon Squeezy checkout page for the purchase of a license.  The purchaser immediately receives from Lemon Sqeezy a license key by email, and enters that key in the application to unlock its full features.  Each purchase includes up to three device activations.
The limited free-tier allows is beneficial to both the customer who can evaluate the app as much as it wants. From our perspective, such an approach reduces considerably the financial risk of a returns. For this reason, we do not offer refunds.
Intended customers
TotalRecalls is designed for individual users who rely on AI assistants daily and want a permanent, portable, local archive of their conversations for:
* Data ownership.
* Privacy.
* Long-term access.
* Searchability.
* Portability between AI providers.
Purchase and delivery model
TotalRecalls is sold through a single purchase only. There are no subscriptions or recurring charges.
Delivery is entirely digital. After purchase, the customer receives a license key by email through Lemon Squeezy.
