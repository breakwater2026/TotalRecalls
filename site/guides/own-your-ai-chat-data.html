Own your AI chat data

AI chats are becoming a second brain. If that brain only exists inside someone else’s web app, you don’t own it — you’re renting access to your own words.

Three risks of “leaving it in the tab”

  • Product risk — features, pricing, and UIs change
  • Account risk — lockouts, plan changes, lost access
  • Tool sprawl — ChatGPT + Claude + Perplexity + Gemini + Grok = fragmented memory

What ownership looks like

Files on your disk. Open formats. Backups you control. That’s it. Not a new cloud silo with another password.
