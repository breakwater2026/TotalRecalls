How it works

1
Download and unzip

Click the blue button above. Open the zip file and double-click TotalRecalls.exe.

2
Choose an AI app

Pick ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini, or Grok from the list in the app.

3
Sign in (or point to a Google Takeout folder for Gemini)

A small window opens so you can sign in as yourself — the same way you would in a browser.

4
Export

Choose a folder and start export. When it finishes, open the folder to see your chats.

Common questions

Do I need a GitHub account?

No. Just use the Download button on this page. The file may be stored on our release server; you do not need to know or visit GitHub.

Do you store my ChatGPT / Claude password?

No. Login stays on your PC. We do not offer “sign in with TotalRecalls cloud” and we do not collect provider passwords or session cookies on our servers.

Windows says the app is unrecognized

That is normal until we add a paid code-signing certificate. Use More info → Run anyway when you downloaded from this official site. On some locked-down PCs (corporate or “Smart App Control”), unsigned apps cannot run at all.

What about Gemini?

For Gemini, use a Google Takeout export folder (or JSON file). In the app, choose Gemini and paste or browse to that folder — this is more reliable than browser tricks.

Mac or phone?

Windows first. Mac and other platforms may come later.

Is this free forever?

The current public beta is free to download. We plan a simple one-time price (about $19–29) when the product is polished and signed — not a monthly subscription.
