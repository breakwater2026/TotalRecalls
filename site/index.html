How it works

1
Pick a provider

Perplexity, ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini (Google Takeout path), or Grok.

2
Sign in on your machine

Use the embedded window, paste a session token, or point Gemini at a Takeout export.

3
Export to disk

Readable Markdown plus machine-friendly JSON under your chosen folder.

Honest posture

TotalRecalls is a user-driven, on-device export utility. You authenticate as yourself; files stay local. Providers also offer official data-export paths that can take longer — we complement those when you need your chats now in a clean, searchable archive. Not affiliated with OpenAI, Perplexity, Anthropic, Google, or xAI.
