Honest posture

TotalRecalls is a user-driven, on-device export tool. You authenticate as yourself; files stay local. Providers also offer official data-export paths that can take longer — we complement those when you need your chats now in a clean, searchable archive. One-time utility pricing planned (~$19–29); core development is open on GitHub.
