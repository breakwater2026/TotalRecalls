Privacy

Last updated: 2026-08-10

In plain language

TotalRecalls is a Windows app on your computer. It helps you copy your own AI chat history into folders on your disk. It is not a website that stores your conversations for you.

What stays on your computer

  • Anything you type when signing into ChatGPT, Perplexity, and other services inside the app
  • Temporary session information the app needs to talk to those services
  • The exported chat files you choose to save
  • Optional local log files used only for troubleshooting on your PC

What we do not do

  • We do not ask you to create a TotalRecalls cloud account to export chats
  • We do not upload your provider passwords or session cookies to TotalRecalls servers
  • We do not sell your chat content

This website

The marketing site (totalrecalls.app) is a normal product website: pages, download links, and privacy text. If we add analytics later, we will update this page. Download files may be delivered from our release hosting; clicking Download does not require a GitHub account.
