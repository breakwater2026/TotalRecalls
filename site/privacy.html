Privacy

Last updated: 2026-08-10

What TotalRecalls is

TotalRecalls is a desktop application that helps you export your own AI chat history to folders on your computer. It is not a cloud service that stores your conversations for you.

What stays on your computer

  • Session tokens / cookies used to talk to providers (if you sign in inside the app)
  • Exported Markdown and JSON files you choose to save
  • Local app logs under your user profile (for troubleshooting)

What we do not collect

  • We do not operate a TotalRecalls login backend for provider accounts
  • We do not receive or store your provider session tokens on our servers
  • We do not sell chat content

Network traffic

When you export, the app contacts the AI provider you selected (for example Perplexity, OpenAI/ChatGPT, Anthropic/Claude, Google Takeout files on disk, or xAI/Grok) using credentials you supplied on your machine. Website analytics on totalrecalls.app, if any are added later, will be documented here.
