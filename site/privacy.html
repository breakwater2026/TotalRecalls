Privacy

TotalRecalls is built around local ownership of your AI conversations.

What the Windows app does

  • Runs on your computer
  • Exports chats to folders you choose
  • Uses local login windows or a Gemini Takeout path you provide
  • Does not require a TotalRecalls account to store your chats in our cloud — we don’t operate that product

What we don’t want

  • Your ChatGPT/Claude/Perplexity session cookies on our servers
  • A hosted database of your private threads

This website

The marketing site is static pages. Standard server logs may include IPs and URLs (Cloud Run / host). We don’t run AdSense on this product site.

Payments

Checkout is handled by Lemon Squeezy (merchant of record). Their privacy policy applies to payment data. We receive order information needed to fulfill your purchase (e.g. email, product).

Contact

Support: use the email on your Lemon receipt or the contact method listed when checkout goes live.
